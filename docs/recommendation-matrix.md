# Which Mechanism: the recommendation matrix

Given a workload, which of the five **Mechanisms** does the evidence support, and where does it say nothing?

Vocabulary is the glossary's (`CONTEXT.md`). A **Mechanism** is one of five interchangeable ways of claiming Seats: unique constraint, pessimistic locking, optimistic locking, advisory locks, serializable isolation. The **Control** (`naive`, the unguarded read-then-write) is not a Mechanism; it is a reference column. The **Backstop** is the `UNIQUE(seat_id)` constraint the shared schema puts beneath every Mechanism and the Control (ADR-0008). **Every measurement below except Exhibit I ran with the Backstop in place**, so "zero double-bookings" there says the combination holds, not that a Mechanism holds alone.

## The default

**Use `pessimistic` (`SELECT … FOR UPDATE NOWAIT`) unless a row of the matrix says otherwise.**

1. **It is the only Mechanism with the hold → confirm → expire flow.** The flow exists for `pessimistic` alone and `POST /holds` answers 404 under any other Mechanism ([ADR-0017](adr/0017-phase5-scoped-to-pessimistic-only.md), [ADR-0019](adr/0019-holds-gated-by-booking-strategy.md)). Across 3 repeats of 200 expired Holds raced by Confirms, fresh Holds and the reaper: 0 late-succeeding confirms, 0 permanently stuck Seats, 600 of 600 Seats re-claimed ([F-Results](evidence/exhibit-f-phase5-hold-expiry.md)).
2. **It has no isolation finding.** A hot Seat changed unrelated Seats' p99 by −13.6% at 5 repeats, a PASS against the 10% limit (NFR2). `optimistic` is the one Mechanism that failed, at +60.1% ([E-Results](evidence/exhibit-e-phase4-isolation.md)). That PASS holds under the widened connection configuration only: under the project's earlier, smaller pool the same measurement read +374.7%, which Exhibit E attributes to connection-pool queuing, not to the Mechanism ([E-Results, negative control](evidence/exhibit-e-phase4-isolation.md)).
3. **It held without the Backstop, in these runs.** 6 of 6 runs held and every Booking was served: one per hot-seat run, 30 per overlapping-pairs run (3 of 3 and 90 of 90 across 3 repeats) ([I-Results](evidence/exhibit-i-phase8-no-backstop.md)). That is 3 repeats of 60 concurrent requests: evidence at that load, not a proof.
4. **It cannot deadlock by construction.** Non-waiting acquisition has no wait-for edges whatever the order of the Seats; the model checks that this protocol cannot cycle while blocking acquisition in client order deadlocks in 3 steps ([G-DeadlockAB](evidence/exhibit-g-phase6-formal.md)). The formal models are of its claim protocol and hold lifecycle; the other Mechanisms are modelled only as the shared atomic claim and their deadlock shape ([G-Headline, and What this does not prove, item 3](evidence/exhibit-g-phase6-formal.md)).

**What the default costs, and what it does not beat.**

- On the zero-contention pattern its p99 is 168.0 ms, second-highest of the six (only `serializable`, 258.5 ms, is higher; `optimistic` is 90.5 ms). Single runs from before the later fixes, which made that path consistently slower by an amount nobody could separate from noise ([C-Results](evidence/exhibit-c-phase2-comparison.md); see the provenance table).
- On one hot Seat at 1,000 concurrent requests it posted the highest throughput of the six in Exhibit C's run (5,685.1 req/s, p99 105.3 ms; `advisory` 5,670.3, within 0.3%). **That ranking did not hold up in a later rerun**: Exhibit H re-measured the same cell and `pessimistic` read 3,752 req/s at p99 252.8 ms, against 6,972 for `unique_constraint` and 5,968 for `advisory`, with 548 of its 2,000 requests lost to load-generator connection resets ([H-G3](evidence/exhibit-h-phase6-chaos.md)). Read the hot-seat numbers as "all six in the same range", not as a ranking.
- The evidence does not separate `pessimistic` from `advisory`. `advisory` also passed isolation (−2.1%, 2 repeats) and held without the Backstop, though it left one of 90 Bookings unserved in one run ([E-Results](evidence/exhibit-e-phase4-isolation.md), [I-Results](evidence/exhibit-i-phase8-no-backstop.md)). The default rests on the hold flow, the larger evidence base and the absence of a caveat, not on a measured win over `advisory`.

### Reconciled with Exhibit I

The working candidate was `pessimistic`; Exhibit I does not change it. The prediction was that the Control and the unique-constraint Mechanism fail without the Backstop and the other four hold. Observed: the four coordinated Mechanisms held in 24 of 24 runs and the Control double-booked in 6 of 6, **as predicted**. The unique-constraint Mechanism did **not** double-book as predicted: it was inoperative in 6 of 6 runs, booking nothing and answering HTTP 500, because its `ON CONFLICT (seat_id)` insert needs the unique index to exist. The prediction was missed; the conclusion it was after, that it cannot stand alone, was not ([I-Results](evidence/exhibit-i-phase8-no-backstop.md)). By the glossary's own definition the unique-constraint Mechanism is the Backstop with nothing in front of it, so it is the layer beneath the other four, not an alternative to them. That `pessimistic` stays the default is this document's reading; Exhibit I does not itself make a recommendation.

Two limits, both Exhibit I's own: it ran at 60 concurrent requests, not 1,000 (the chaos harness could not carry 1,000 on this machine), and it exercised booking claims only, so **`pessimistic`'s hold/confirm flow without the Backstop is not measured** ([I-Limits](evidence/exhibit-i-phase8-no-backstop.md)).

### Terms used above

- **NFR2**: the requirement that a hot Seat degrade p99 latency for unrelated Seats by under 10%.
- **C2 matrix**: Exhibit H's runs of every Mechanism and the Control under five injected Faults (latency below and above the client timeout, partition stall and reset, application SIGKILL) and two loads, `hammer-seat` (one Seat) and `deadlock-pairs` (overlapping two-Seat pairs).
- **R4, R6, R7, RF1, RF2** (Exhibit H's Robustness findings, all fixed or decided): R4 no database timeouts; R6 retry loops under a slow link; R7 a stalled reaper; RF1 an Idempotency Key outliving its Booking; RF2 a spurious 409 on a same-key retry.
- **Oracle-clean**: no Correctness violation found by the Oracles that read stored state.

### Reading the matrix

- **Where it tells you not to take the default.** If a hot Seat must not slow others, avoid `optimistic` (the one measured FAIL). If overlapping multi-Seat requests dominate and the database link is degraded, `serializable` answered most with 503. The unique-constraint Mechanism is not an alternative to the other four; it is the layer beneath them.
- **Where it does not separate the Mechanisms.** Oracle results under Faults are clean for all six (540 measured runs including post-fix reruns, zero Correctness violations, [H-Headline](evidence/exhibit-h-phase6-chaos.md)), so correctness under Faults does not rank them.
- **Negative degradations are not speed-ups.** Exhibit E's negative values (down to −63.4%) come from 2 repeats. Only `pessimistic`'s first figure (+37.5% at 2 repeats) was shown to be noise, by a 5-repeat re-measurement that gave −13.6%; `unique_constraint`, `advisory`, `serializable` and the Control were not re-run, so their size and sign are unconfirmed. Of the six, only `pessimistic` and `optimistic` were re-measured at 5 repeats, and only `optimistic`'s failure reproduced ([E-Method](evidence/exhibit-e-phase4-isolation.md)).

## Not measured: no verdict

These carry **no recommendation**: no exhibit measures them, and this list is the edge of the evidence, not a to-do list implying an answer.

- **Multi-node application servers.** Every exhibit is one app process against one containerized Postgres on one machine.
- **Very large venues.** Nothing beyond the seeded Seat counts of the load patterns was tried.
- **Power loss, or storage that lies about `fsync`.** A killed Postgres container lost no acknowledged commit, but it keeps the Docker VM's page cache, so a machine crash is untested ([H-N5](evidence/exhibit-h-phase6-chaos.md)).
- **The actor / per-Seat-queue variant.** Not built and not measured: a scope decision, not an omission.
- **Hold/confirm for any Mechanism but `pessimistic`, and for `pessimistic` without the Backstop.**
- **Mixed workloads** (a hot Seat and overlapping multi-Seat requests together), **Cancel under load**, and **other hardware or pool sizes.** The PRD's own risk note stands: results are sensitive to hardware and connection-pool configuration.
